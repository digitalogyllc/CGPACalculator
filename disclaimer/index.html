2. Calculator accuracy and limitations

Our CGPA-to-percentage calculator uses formulas that are publicly published by various Indian universities, the CBSE board, and other recognized educational institutions. While we work to keep these formulas up to date, please be aware of the following:

  • Formula changes: Universities occasionally revise their official conversion formulas. There may be a delay between such a change and when our calculator reflects it.
  • Department-level variations: Within a single university, individual departments or programs sometimes apply minor adjustments to the standard formula.
  • Grading scale assumptions: Our calculator assumes you know which grading scale (10-point, 5-point, 4-point) your transcript uses. If you select the wrong one, the result will be incorrect.
  • Approximation, not equivalence: CGPA-to-percentage conversion is inherently a statistical approximation, not a literal one-to-one mapping. Different students with the same CGPA may have earned slightly different raw percentages.
  • Rounding: Our calculator displays results rounded to two decimal places. Your university may use different rounding conventions.
