What is a Good CGPA in India? The Complete Benchmarks Guide

From recruiters to government exams, here's what 7, 8, and 9 CGPA actually mean in the real world — backed by placement data and admission cutoffs.

E
CGPA/P Editorial
· March 5, 2026· 6 min read

A CGPA of 8.0 or above (equivalent to 76%+) is considered good in India. A CGPA above 9.0 is excellent and opens doors to top tier-1 companies, premier PG programs, and competitive scholarships. A CGPA between 7.0 and 7.9 is decent and qualifies you for most job applications, while below 6.0 may restrict your options.

Context matters: 7.5 from IIT is more valuable than 9.5 from an unknown college, and engineering CGPA bands differ from arts/commerce expectations.

CGPA for Campus Placements

Most multinational corporations (MNCs) and product-based companies set a minimum CGPA cutoff for campus recruitment. Here's the typical landscape:

  • Tier-1 companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs): 8.0-9.0 CGPA minimum, with most actual offers going to candidates above 8.5
  • Tier-2 IT services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini): 6.0-7.0 CGPA minimum, no upper restriction
  • Core engineering companies (L&T, Bosch, Siemens): 7.5+ CGPA
  • Consulting firms (BCG, Bain, McKinsey): 8.5+ CGPA plus strong communication skills
  • Startups and product companies: Often less CGPA-focused, more skill-and-portfolio focused
The 60% Rule

Most Indian companies require minimum 60% in 10th, 12th, and graduation — even if your CGPA is high in college. A 6.5 CGPA (61.75%) is the practical floor for the broadest set of opportunities.

CGPA cutoffs at most Indian companies fall between 6.0 and 8.5.

CGPA for Government Jobs

Most government job notifications (UPSC, SSC, banking, railways, PSUs) state eligibility in percentage rather than CGPA. The typical requirements:

  • UPSC Civil Services: Just a graduation degree — no minimum marks specified
  • SSC CGL: Graduation in any discipline (no minimum percentage)
  • Bank PO / SBI PO: 60% aggregate in graduation (CGPA 6.3+)
  • PSU jobs (ONGC, BHEL, IOCL via GATE): 60-65% in engineering (CGPA 6.3 - 6.85)
  • RBI Grade B: 60% in graduation

The percentage floor of 60% maps to roughly 6.3 CGPA on the standard scale, making this the safest target for keeping government job options open.

CGPA for Scholarships

Most merit-based scholarships have explicit CGPA cutoffs:

  • INSPIRE Scholarship (DST): Top 1% in 12th board + 7.0 CGPA in B.Sc.
  • UGC NET-JRF: 55% master's CGPA (5.5+)
  • Tata Scholarship: 7.0+ CGPA
  • Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship: 60% in 12th
  • Fulbright (US study): Strong undergraduate record, typically 8.0+ CGPA

Why Context Matters More Than Numbers

CGPA is not read in a vacuum. Recruiters and admissions panels evaluate your CGPA alongside:

  • Institution tier — A 7.5 from IIT Bombay carries more weight than a 9.5 from a tier-3 college
  • Branch/specialization — CSE and ECE typically have lower median CGPAs than mechanical or civil; recruiters know this
  • Trajectory — Did your CGPA improve over semesters? An upward curve from 7.0 to 8.5 reads stronger than flat 7.8
  • Other achievements — Internships, research, hackathon wins, and projects often outweigh raw CGPA

So the honest answer is: a good CGPA is one that, combined with the rest of your profile, gets you to your target. For most students aiming at average outcomes, aim for 7.5+; if you're chasing tier-1 placements or top PG programs, aim for 8.5+. Frequently Asked Questions

Is 7 CGPA good in India?
A 7 CGPA (66.5%) is decent and qualifies you for most IT service company placements and many government jobs (which usually need 60%+). However, it falls below the cutoff for top-tier companies and competitive PG programs.
Is 8 CGPA enough for placement?
Yes. An 8.0 CGPA (76%) clears the cutoff for almost all campus recruiters in India, including most MNCs. Tier-1 product companies may set their bar at 8.5, but 8.0 is solidly placement-ready.
What is 9 CGPA in percentage?
9.0 CGPA equals 85.5% using the standard formula (9.0 × 9.5). This is considered an excellent score and puts you in the top 5-10% of your class.
Is 6.5 CGPA bad?
6.5 CGPA (61.75%) clears the 60% minimum required by most companies and government jobs. It's not bad, but it limits access to top-tier placements and competitive PG admissions.
Can I get into IIT M.Tech with 7 CGPA?
Yes, the typical minimum CGPA for IIT M.Tech via GATE is 6.5-7.0. However, your final selection depends heavily on your GATE score and interview performance.
